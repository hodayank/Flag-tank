==================================================
PART 1. CONCEPT AND NON-NEGOTIABLE SCOPE
==================================================
Two human players on two separate devices on the same local Wi-Fi, one tank each, race for ONE shared moving golden flag. It is NOT base defense, NOT capture-the-base, NOT a campaign. Any flag fort, pylons, or extra red tanks in the images are illustrative only. Exactly three themes: Najdi Saudi village, Classic/Basic red-brick, Japanese countryside. An optional collectible helper tank is the only extra friendly unit. No ads, monetization, chat, accounts, cloud backend, internet matchmaking, stores, or external links.

PART 2. ART DIRECTION (match the images exactly in quality, shape language, material feel, lighting, camera, mood)
==================================================
2.1 GLOBAL LOOK
Premium stylized "toy diorama" 3D, mobile-game quality (Supercell-like polish). Chunky, rounded, softly beveled, tactile, like hand-made miniatures. Warm golden daylight, saturated but harmonious colors, soft contact shadows, gentle ambient occlusion in crevices. No pixel art, no flat vector, no photoreal noise, no black shadows hiding paths, no heavy bloom, no gore.
Materials: matte dusty mud/adobe with dry grain; porous warm terracotta brick with mortar lines; worn grainy timber with visible planks; ceramic pots; dark rubber tank tracks; softly painted tank metal with light edge wear (NOT glossy featureless plastic); cloth with soft folds. Roughness variation, small normal detail, hand-painted tint variation per block (no two blocks identical).
Lighting: one soft sun from the upper-left of the frame, shadows falling to lower-right, warm ambient fill. No day/night cycle.
Color: warm cream/sand dominant for ground (about #E9C98F to #D9A760), clean saturated accents: team blue, team red, gold, leaf green, water blue.

2.2 CAMERA
Fixed orthographic overhead-tilted camera about 55 degrees above ground, slight 3/4 view so wall fronts and tops are both visible, arena edges roughly parallel to screen edges, landscape. Playable maze fills about 80-85% of view with a rich narrow decorative perimeter. View is from Player 1's side (spawn bottom, rival spawn at the opposite top-right corner); Player 2 sees the same world rotated 180 degrees so their own tank is also at the bottom. Walls about 0.85 m high so they never hide tanks; boundary about 1.2 m. Camera angle, target, bounds are config values.

PART 4. GAME RULES (all numbers in versioned, validated JSON; every value below is an adjustable default)
==================================================

No protected flag base. Deterministic generator: blocked by default except odd/odd corridors and cells where (col + 3*row) % 7 == 0; mirror under (26-col, 16-row); clear spawn pockets and connect them; add reviewed plugs to reach 45-55% destructible density; export frozen JSON; tests reject invalid overlaps, out-of-bounds and unwinnable layouts. Three map files (najdi-01, basic-01, japan-01) with the SAME logical layout first so theme never changes difficulty. Map schema: id, revision, cellSizeM, cols, rows, spawns, walls[{cell, assetId, hp}], boundary[{assetId, position, yaw}], flagCandidates, powerupCandidates, decor[{id, assetId, position, yaw, scale, animation?}].
- Match: 180 s default, selectable 300 s, higher score wins. Tie goes to sudden death: clear active flag and pending spawn, spawn a fresh fair deciding flag after a brief shared transition, first valid pickup wins, safety timeout 60 s then draw. No extra damage advantage.
- Flag: exactly one active; +1 point exactly once on pickup at its location (never carried back); new flag after 1.3 s at a fair, reachable, path-cost-aware location (accounting for breakable-wall cost, symmetric candidates, not inside colliders or tanks, not repeatedly beside the leader); authority chooses and broadcasts; if no candidate, relax the documented threshold or use a safe center tile, never freeze. Helpers cannot collect flags. No invented flag lifetime. Score survives tank disable.
- Manual fire: tap/key-down fires one shot with 0.16 s minimum interval; held fire repeats every 0.43 s; one lastShotTime plus requested-mode interval (a rapid tap is never blocked by a full held cooldown); OS key repeat is not counted as taps; no default auto-fire. Swept/sub-stepped projectile collision, first valid collider, no tunneling; muzzle starting inside a wall blocks the shot.
- Health: tank 100 HP, damage 25, floating real applied damage. At 0 HP: brief non-violent disable, combat collision removed, returns to own spawn after 3 s with 100 HP and 2 s protection that ends the moment it fires. Never respawn into a collider or tank; wait or use a small reviewed fallback region. No lives, elimination or kill bonus.
- Walls: HP in config (default 2 ordinary hits: intact, cracked, destroyed; the owner mentioned 3-4 hits verbally, so keep this a prominent tunable and ask for the final value). Cracked state persists (no healing). Destruction opens passage immediately; rubble is visual only. Rebuild starts 10 s after full destruction, with 2.5 s ghost telegraph, postponed while a tank/helper occupies footprint plus 0.15 m margin; rebuild restores HP, collider and visuals on the same authoritative tick; fixed ordering of projectile vs rebuild; never trap or crush units; never count a resolved projectile twice.
- Helper pickup: max one active per team, independent AI with line-of-sight (no wall vision, no guaranteed hits), break-cost-aware pathing, avoids deadlock beside owner, 50 HP (derived from damage x 2 hits), moveSpeedMultiplier 0.85, no automatic respawn, lifetime null by default (no hidden expiry), cannot collect flags. Friendly bullets pass through friendly human/helper tanks; enemy bullets hit whichever is first. A second helper pickup while one is active does not stack (pick one consistent policy and document it). Spawn at a free nearby navigable cell.
- Pickups (shared, weighted, no host advantage): first spawn 5 s, every 10 s, lifetime 18 s, max 2 visible, only in legal reachable cells, postponed if no safe tile. Shield: absorbs 2 enemy projectiles or 5 s. Rapid fire: interval divided by 1.15 for 8 s. Strong rocket: next 2 shots have two wall-hit strength, no homing, tank damage stays 25, expires after 8 s. Auto fire: automatic ordinary shots at held cadence for 8 s, then back to manual. Helper: spawns one helper. Only one main-tank buff at a time (new different buff replaces old with feedback); helper may coexist; main buff and shield clear on main-tank disable; helper survives. No laser, repair kit, tracking rockets, permanent armies.
- Input: mobile left virtual joystick plus separate large right fire button with independent pointer IDs, simultaneous move and fire; no browser scroll/zoom during play; release on pointercancel, lost capture, blur, background and host pause. Desktop WASD/arrows plus Space; optional clearly-marked dev same-keyboard mode IJKL plus Enter. Heading follows movement, keeps last heading when stopped, smooth bounded turn rate, analog 360 motion, normalized diagonals, collision sliding never increases speed or clips corners, turn-in-place supported. All velocities in seconds on fixed sim time. No twin-stick or auto-aim.

PART 7. AUTHORITY, PROTOCOL AND DETERMINISM
==================================================
The room creator runs the only authoritative simulation; the guest sends validated input intents only (never scores, positions, damage or wall changes). Sender identity comes from the transport and RoomState, never from payload fields. Rates (configurable): sim 30 Hz, input 20 Hz plus discrete fire edges, snapshots 10 Hz plus reliable state-change events, interpolation 100 ms, input timeout 250 ms (stale input becomes zero; pause is separate from timeout). If only reliable delivery exists, coalesce inputs/snapshots instead of growing a queue; negotiate a documented fallback if "latest" is unsupported. Full snapshot on join/reconnect and periodically.
Envelope {protocol 'tank-flags/1', roomId, matchId, sequence, tick, type, payload}. InputPayload {inputSequence, moveX, moveZ, fireHeld, tapSequence}. SnapshotPayload {acknowledgedInputs, remainingTicks, phase countdown|playing|paused|sudden-death|ended, tanks[{id, ownerId, team, helper, x, z, heading, hp, disabledUntilTick, protectedUntilTick, buff}], projectiles[{id, team, x, z, heading, kind}], walls[{cellIndex, hp, rebuildAtTick}], flag, pickups[{id, cellIndex, kind, expiresAtTick}], scores, lastEventSequence}. GameEventPayload {eventId, kind (shot, tank-hit, tank-disabled, respawn, wall-cracked, wall-destroyed, wall-rebuilt, flag-spawned, flag-collected, pickup-collected, buff-expired, helper-spawned, match-ended), data} with fully typed, validated, discriminated data (no "unknown" in production). Validate numeric ranges, lengths, tick/sequence sanity, room/match membership and entity IDs; 64 KiB packet cap and aggregate rate limit; reject stale-match packets; malformed packets never crash or mutate state; a client can never claim thousands of taps in one packet.
Tick order: accept legal inputs, movement/collision, eligible shots, sweep projectiles, damage/disable, flag/pickup collection, timers/rebuild, match end check, emit events and snapshot, with stable explicit tie ordering.
